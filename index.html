Battle Standard Bearer

A single Gate Keeper in your army may be upgraded to be your Battle Standard Bearer for +25 pts. In addition to their usual allowance of points to spend on runic items a Battle Standard Bearer can purchase Standard Runes with no point limit.

Magic Items

Any model within this army that have the option to purchase magic items may purchase magic items from the Grand Cathay and common item list.



































Stone Army Special Rules

Army Made of Stone and Flesh

Under the rules of the game your army Must include 2 generals, 1 of Stone(Shogun/Sentinel) and 1 normal(each worth aditional 50 points instead of 100 when killed). Stone general works for Unbreakable units, while Normal works for others.

Will of the Stone

Your Unbreakable units gain Will of the Dragons special rule.

Magic of the Stone

Your army's Battle Standard Bearer and your Stone General are now a level 1 Elementalism or Battle Magic wizard with lore of Yin or Yang

Sentilel Legends

Your Cathayan Sentinels in Character slots can take magic items worth up to 50 points and they don't have to be general even with highest Leadership
